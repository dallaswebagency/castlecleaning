Questions you may have

New to house cleaning? Never be afraid to ask us more. Communication is key to us doing the best job possible for you.

See all questions
I'm so embarrassed to have someone see my home!

Try not to worry. We're not here to judge, we're here to help. Life gets in the way sometimes and we've seen many messes. We love cleaning and hope you'll trust us with yours.

Do I need to supply my own vacuum, mop etc.?

We ask that you supply your own toilet brush for sanitary purposes. The rest is up to you. If you have pets, we prefer you supply the vacuum, mop and broom so we don't transfer pet fur to homes where there may be allergies.

Are you pet friendly?

Yes, we love animals! Tell us about your pet before our first visit. We only ask that aggressive animals, or ones scared of the vacuum, be out of the house while we clean.

Do you clean homes that have been smoked in?

We do not clean smoked in homes, as we value our health and the health of our employees.

Should I be at home when you come to clean?

No, it's up to you. If you're away, provide a spare key, hide one on the property, or give us a garage or door code.

How do I pay for the cleaning?

We bill after each clean, by emailed invoice or a paper invoice left at your house. You can pay by cheque, cash or e-transfer.

What happens if I'm not happy with the clean?

Let us know as soon as possible if we missed or messed up anything. We'll come back and fix it as soon as we're able.
